How it works

Four moving parts. Setup takes about ten minutes.

1. Point your number

Forward your existing line, or port it. Nothing changes for your callers.

2. Missed call → text

No answer, busy, or declined: the caller gets a text in ~8 seconds, in your voice.

3. Qualify + book

It asks what's wrong, how urgent, and drops a booking link. You get a summary.
