# Go-to-market: first 10 customers

Everything below is copy you can paste. The only parts that need you: your name, your
number, and hitting send.

## Who to target

Home-service businesses, 1–10 vans, that already run Google Ads (if they spend on ads,
they already believe leads have a price — that's the whole sale).

Find them in ~20 minutes:
- Google Maps: "plumber" / "hvac" / "electrician" + your city. Skip franchises (Podium owns them).
- Look for: a "Call now" button, a contact form, no online booking, reviews mentioning
  "couldn't get through" or "never called back". Those are your best leads.
- Google Ads Transparency Center → filter by region → see who's paying. Ads + no booking = perfect.

## The email (send 20/day, 5 days, one niche)

Subject: `missed call yesterday?`

> Hi {First},
>
> I called {Business} yesterday around 2pm and got voicemail — figured you were on a job.
>
> That's the thing I wanted to ask about: how many calls a week ring out while you're
> under a sink? Most shops I talk to say 10–20. At $400 a job, that's real money walking
> to the next guy on Google.
>
> I built a small thing that texts those callers back in about 8 seconds, asks what's
> wrong, and books them in. Want to see it run on your number for two weeks? Free, no
> card, cancel by text.
>
> — {Name}
> {phone}

Why it works: it proves you actually called, it names a number they recognize, and the
ask is a trial, not a demo call.

## Follow-ups (most replies come from these)

**+2 days:** `Re: missed call yesterday?` — "Bumping this once. I have two spots left for
the free two-week run this month. Want one?"

**+5 days:** `Re: missed call yesterday?` — "Last note from me — I recorded a 40-second
video of your missed call getting texted back automatically. Want the link?"

## The 40-second Loom (record once, reuse forever)

1. Dial their number on screen. Let it ring out.
2. Cut to your phone: the text-back arrives.
3. Reply as the customer: "water heater leaking, can someone come today?"
4. Show the AI reply + booked slot.
5. Close: "This took 8 seconds. Want it on your line for two weeks, free?"

That video does more selling than any deck.

## The phone call (if you'd rather call)

> "Hey {First}, this is {Name} — I'm not selling you marketing, I'm calling because I
> rang your number yesterday and got voicemail. Quick question: when that happens, does
> anyone text that caller back? … Right, most don't. I set up a thing that does it
> automatically in about 8 seconds and books the job. Want me to turn it on for two weeks
> so you can see the calls you're missing? No charge."

## Pricing conversation

Never discount the price; discount the risk.

- "First 14 days free. After that it's $299/mo, cancel by text."
- If they hesitate: "If it doesn't recover at least one job in two weeks, don't pay me."
- Raise price after customer #5. You will be shocked how little price resistance there is
  when the product is tied to revenue.

## The honest math

20 emails/day × 5 days = 100 emails. Realistic: 5–8 replies, 1–3 trials, 1 customer.
Repeat weekly for a month → 4–8 customers → $1.2–2.4k MRR. That is the entire game:
volume of boring, specific outreach. There is no shortcut and no clever hack that replaces it.
